# /bin/diff.py
**Description:** Compares two files line by line and outputs the differences in a standard diff format.
**Usage:** `/bin/diff.py <file1_path> <file2_path>`
**Example:** `/bin/diff.py /doc/qa/ask.md /doc/qa/cat.md`
**Output:** Shows a diff between the two specified files, highlighting added, deleted, and changed lines. The output for `/doc/qa/ask.md` and `/doc/qa/cat.md` correctly showed their differences.
**Status:** Working.
