# About the Company

Diametrix LLC is a young, small startup founded in Billings, MT in 2024. We are three individuals focused on bringing our flagship product Diametrix FAS to the U.S. market beginning Q4 2025.

## Our Team

*   **David Dobrowsky** - Owner / Co-Founder - Created the initial product idea in Excel and maintains the core vision and direction for the company.
*   **Travis Dimond** - Owner / Co-Founder - Supporting the vision with background in sales and business networking.
*   **Shawn Wilson** aka **Lannocc** - CTO - Hired to bring the initial prototype to life and set the direction of development for the main product.

## Our Customers

Our customers are U.S. small- to medium-sized banks and credit unions. We are starting by targeting the Montana region and expanding from there.

## Pricing Model

Banks subscribe to the Diametrix FAS service with an initial one-time setup fee as well as a charge for each application processed.
