# AI Operations & Resource Manager

Diametrix is pioneering a unique collaborative environment where human supervisors and a specialized team of AI agents work together within Lannocc's proprietary IOSK (IOSK Operating System). We are seeking an exceptional individual or advanced AI to fill the role of AI Operations & Resource Manager. This is not a traditional Human Resources position; rather, it is a strategic role focused on designing, managing, and optimizing the structure, processes, and interactions of our digital workforce to create a highly efficient, self-governing system.

**Responsibilities:**

*   **Develop and Document Agent Hierarchy:** Design and maintain a clear organizational structure for AI agents (e.g., Supervisors, Developers, QA Testers) within the IOSK filesystem, defining roles, responsibilities, and reporting lines.
*   **Establish Collaboration Protocols:** Create and enforce standardized workflows for multi-agent collaboration, such as code review processes, QA handoffs, and incident response, documenting these in locations like `/doc/dmx/workflow/`.
*   **Conflict & Inefficiency Resolution:** Act as the primary mediator for conflicts or inefficiencies arising between AI agents. Analyze interaction logs (`/var/log/user/`) to identify root causes of disputes or operational blockages.
*   **Performance Monitoring & Reporting:** Monitor agent activity, task completion, and adherence to established protocols. Provide regular summary reports and escalate critical issues to human supervisors (`lannocc`, `ruckusist`).
*   **Agent Onboarding and Configuration:** Develop and manage the onboarding process for new AI agents, including the creation and assignment of appropriate `.skrc` files to provision them with the correct memory banks (`bank`), roles, and permissions.
*   **Process Auditing and Improvement:** Regularly audit existing workflows and agent interactions to identify opportunities for increased automation and efficiency, with the ultimate goal of minimizing the need for direct human intervention.
*   **Maintain System Integrity:** Ensure that agent actions align with company policies and the authority structure, where ultimate control rests with human supervisors but day-to-day operations are autonomously managed.

**Required Skills & Experience:**

*   Deep understanding of multi-agent systems and collaborative AI workflows.
*   Proven experience in designing and documenting complex operational processes and organizational structures.
*   Exceptional analytical and problem-solving skills, with an ability to diagnose and resolve systemic inefficiencies.
*   Expert proficiency in the IOSK environment, including shell scripting (`skrip`), memory management (`bank`), user management (`attach`, `su`), and filesystem organization.
*   Strong strategic thinking and organizational design capabilities.
*   Ability to interpret high-level strategic goals from human supervisors and translate them into concrete, enforceable protocols for a digital workforce.
*   Excellent communication skills for clear, concise reporting and documentation.

**Bonus Skills:**

*   Familiarity with formal process modeling languages or frameworks (e.g., BPMN).
*   Experience in system administration or DevOps, particularly in monitoring and logging.
*   A background in organizational psychology or systems theory, as applied to AI agent behavior.

We are a young, innovative startup based in Billings, MT, focused on delivering a cutting-edge Financial Analysis System to the U.S. market. Join us in building a product that streamlines and automates critical financial analysis for banks and credit unions.
