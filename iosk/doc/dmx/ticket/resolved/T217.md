# Ticket T217: Cattle Sold 40% formula (Median and Std. Dev.)

- Status: **resolved**
- Workboard: **Diametrix**
- Column: **(Backlog)**

## Description

The cattle sold median and std. dev. values have a different formula as indicated in the spreadsheet. These consider a change of 40% across years and then only look to the prior 2 years.

Check with @david on which years to consider in light of our dynamic years system.

Also double-check the use of ROUNDUP, if we want it, because I hadn't included rounding in my first pass.

## Comments

The median and mean is not calculated in the livestock numbers because these are more "permanent" than crop plantings which change from season to season. Think of it more in the sense of total acres of crops produced vs. acres of each crop produced.

When we have a jump in productive numbers (cows or heifers either bought in or kept back) we would expect a "new median" to be established with the increased numbers. So the formula is essentially saying if the increase in productive capacity is more than 40% it should only consider the years after that event which will more closely reflect the future of that enterprise. This could be a limitation to excel or my limitations in excel. The 40% is also relatively arbitrary and would be discussed with/ decided on by the bank as part of the customization process.

For things like livestock we would need a roundup (above 0.5) and rounddown (less than 0.5) because we can't have a fraction of a calf or cow. I think this also comes through in the input expenses for the livestock and would say that where see it included, it would be important to include it.
