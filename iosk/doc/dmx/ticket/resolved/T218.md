## Comments

The total's for each commodity is the sum (column G and R) of each itemized input. As far as the "Total" cost for all commodities is concerned the cost/acre would need to be a weighted average cost as certain crops cost less and or more than others to produce. The totals of the crops is simply the sum of each crop produced as this is fixed and does not change. For example, if you produce 100 acres of corn at $500/acre and 50 acres of Soyabeans at $250/acre and 250 acres of wheat at $100/acre you produce 400 acres of crops, but your average expense isn't $283/acre (($500 + $250 + $100)/3) or $113k but rather $218.75/ acre or $87K. The "total" row (16 and 27) are therefore weighted averages. Because the automatic entries are already an average (schedule F cost / acres produced) the weighted average will be the same as the individual expense.

I had a look at the excel formulas as to why the manual values aren't populating and I think this is an excel bug. (if I change the formula and put it in descending order it seems to work again) I am not sure why that is. 

Considering the acres for the input expenses are (or should be) from the "Assumptions Outputs", as far as the workflow is concerned this should probably be before the "input Assumptions" tab.
