# Ticket T207

- Status: **open**

## Description

The TNW and Working capital columns in the "Ownership in related entities. Information per latest tax years K-1" is not contained in the K-1's or other required information for an individuals application. These columns should only be added and populated when the individual is providing a guarantee to a business loan. 

In our example Fake II LLC would be requesting a loan and Alice is going to guarantee that loan as the owner of Fake II LLC. We analyze Fake II's financials as part of the request with the TNW and Working Capital determined. These figures would then flow through to Alices "Personal Financial Statement" in addition to the K-1 information. We would want to look for differences in key information (ownership, personal information etc.).
