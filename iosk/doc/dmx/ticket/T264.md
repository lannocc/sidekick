- Status: **open**

## Description

Diametrix is committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal information of our customers and business partners. We have created this privacy policy to comprehensively inform individuals about our privacy practices and to demonstrate our firm commitment to privacy. 
Diametrix implements technical and organizational measures to maintain a level of security appropriate to any risks presented with the personal information we process. These measures are to ensure the ongoing integrity and confidentiality of personal information. Please note that no security measure can be 100% secure and security is a business process and not a deliverable per se. We do, however, evaluate and test our chosen measure on a regular basis to protect your personal information in accordance with this policy. 
Diametrix makes use of third-party providers as part of the functioning of our program and has data encrypted in transit (TLS) and encrypted in situ (please elaborate on this a bit), with data not “stored” longer than is required. Once this process is completed your client’s specific data is housed on your financial institution specific login credentials which includes a 2-factor login authentication mechanism.
Diametrix will not store or use your data for longer than is necessary to process your clients information. Upon the completion of the analysis of a customer the file is “closed”  and the information is printed out as a pdf file and a Binary Long Object (BLOB) to be saved on your system of record, and the information is deleted from FAS. This BLOB requires a specific encryption key to allow the data to be “re-upload” into the program, as the basis for the next years analysis. We also have regular backups of the "production" and are creating a redundant system for failover.
