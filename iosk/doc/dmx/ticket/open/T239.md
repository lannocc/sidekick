# Ticket T239: Identity matching

- Status: **open**
- Workboard: **Diametrix**
- Column: **Functionality**

## Description

As mentioned on essentially day 1, we will need a mechanism to "match" identity of two or more documents. Identity may be SSN. Identity may be a property address. Identity may be a business name. All these variations and more should be handled by a unified identity resolution system.
