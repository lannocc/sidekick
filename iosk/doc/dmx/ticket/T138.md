- Status: **resolved**

## Description

On Thursday we should look at setting up an email address at one of the big players that can act as a top-level account holder for certain critical accounts. It's important we use a different provider than the standard that we normally use for our emails (Microsoft) and that it NOT be connected to the domain (diametrixfs.com). This way if the platform (Outlook) goes down or there are issues with the domain (diametrixfs.com) then we can still access critical accounts to make changes. Most importantly, the accounts that control the domain (e.g. GoDaddy) should NOT associated to emails at that domain; from personal experience this creates a difficult catch-22 situation when there is a problem.

I recommend a shared Google (gmail.com) account for this that the core high-level team members can access.
